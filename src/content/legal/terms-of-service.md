Co-Split provides tools for organizing and managing shared expenses
among individuals within workspaces.

The application's current functionality includes:

- **Account Authentication:** Signing in through Google
  authentication.
- **Workspace Management:** Creating, managing, and deleting shared
  expense workspaces.
- **Member Management:** Inviting users and managing workspace
  membership.
- **Expense Recording:** Adding, editing, and deleting expense
  records.
- **Expense Splitting:** Assigning expenses to participating members

not provide banking, lending, money transfer, payment processing, or
